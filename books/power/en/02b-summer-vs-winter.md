# Summer vs Winter

The same outage can be a very different event depending on season. A household should therefore maintain at least a **winter profile** and a **summer profile**.

In winter, heat retention, frozen pipes, darkness, difficult travel and access to safe outdoor locations for generators or cooking can dominate the plan. Batteries may also perform differently in low temperatures. If the home cannot remain safely warm, relocation must be planned in advance.

In summer, refrigeration and food spoilage can become more urgent, drinking-water demand may increase, and some homes can overheat without powered ventilation or cooling. Solar production may be substantially better than in winter, but it is still variable rather than guaranteed.

Where practical, run the power-cut drill once in a cold season and once in a warm season.
