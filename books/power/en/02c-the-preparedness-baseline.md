# The Preparedness Baseline

A power cut is easier to manage when the household already maintains a basic preparedness reserve. The common Ploos Preparedness model covers water, food, heat and clothing, light and energy, information and communications, payments, hygiene, medicines/first aid and household-specific needs.

The Norwegian edition uses Norway's current one-week official preparedness guidance and its national payment recommendations. The European edition does not silently generalise those Norwegian numbers to every country: duration, water quantities and payment arrangements are labelled by jurisdiction.

Cash is treated as one payment fallback rather than the entire payment plan. The toolkit will calculate a household-defined reserve from likely necessary purchases rather than prescribe one universal amount.
