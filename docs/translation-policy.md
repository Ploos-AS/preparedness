# Translation and Localisation Policy

Norwegian and English publications are sibling editions built from a common factual foundation.

They are **not** required to be literal translations.

## Norwegian (`no`)

- Norwegian audience first.
- Norwegian authorities and terminology when jurisdiction matters.
- Natural Norwegian prose over translation fidelity.

## English (`en`)

- European audience first.
- British/International English by default.
- Natural English prose over literal translation.
- National examples must be labelled by country.

## Shared requirements

Both editions should:

- cover the same core learning goals and safety-critical topics;
- draw regulated or reusable facts from shared structured data where practical;
- keep figures, tables and calculations synchronised where they represent common facts;
- preserve source provenance and verification dates;
- avoid claims that a translated national rule applies elsewhere.

Machine translation may assist drafting, but publication requires editorial review. Safety, legal, radio and electrical material must receive explicit factual review after localisation.
