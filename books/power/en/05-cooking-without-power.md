# Cooking Without Power

Plan meals together with the cooking method. A cupboard full of dry food is less useful if every meal requires long cooking times, large amounts of fuel and extra water.

Keep some food that can be eaten cold or needs only minimal heating.

Camping stoves, small gas burners and similar outdoor appliances should be treated as **outdoor cooking equipment** unless the manufacturer explicitly states otherwise. Never move an outdoor combustion appliance indoors simply because the weather is poor.

Your plan should answer in advance where cooking can actually take place safely. This can be straightforward for a house with suitable outdoor space and much harder for an apartment.

Treat fuel as part of the menu: record which meals need heat, approximate cooking time and the fuel they consume, then test a complete emergency meal using the intended equipment.
