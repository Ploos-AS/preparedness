# Example Profiles

These are worked planning patterns rather than prescriptions.

**Apartment — winter:** prioritise clothing/bedding, a warm zone, lighting, communications and battery power. Keep a high proportion of no-cook meals and arrange relocation if the home cannot remain safely warm.

**Apartment — summer:** refrigeration and food spoilage become more prominent; modest battery systems can cover small loads efficiently.

**House with wood heat — winter:** use a warm zone and reserve electrical energy for loads that genuinely require it.

**House with generator — winter:** define critical loads first, operate the generator safely outdoors, plan fuel and maintenance, and use batteries where useful between generator runs.

**House — summer:** refrigeration, pumps and communications may dominate electrical priorities. Solar can contribute strongly without being treated as guaranteed generation.
