# Introduction

A power cut does not need to be dramatic to cause problems. Lighting, heating, cooking, payments, communications and access to information can all be affected at the same time.

This book is about making a household more resilient in a calm, practical way. The aim is not to buy as much equipment as possible. It is to understand what you need, why you need it, and to test the solution before you depend on it.

## After this book

You should be able to make a simple household plan, calculate basic needs, assess backup power and run a realistic power-cut exercise.

The English edition uses European infrastructure and SI units by default. National rules and official guidance are identified by jurisdiction and must be source-verified before publication.
