# M4 — First book bootstrap

The first title is **Når strømmen går / When the Power Goes Out**.

M4 establishes its canonical source under `books/power/` until a dedicated book repository is available.

## Scope

The book is household-focused and practical. It is not a survivalist collapse narrative. The outcome is a tested household power-cut plan.

## Language

- NO: Norway-first.
- EN: Europe-first, British/international English, SI units.
- Localisation is editorial, not blind translation.

## Planned chapters

1. Introduction
2. The first minutes
3. Heat
4. Light
5. Food and water
6. Backup power
7. Information and communications
8. Your plan
9. Worksheets

## Publishing

HTML, EPUB, Kindle and PDF are mandatory outputs through the shared publishing infrastructure.

## Qualification rule

Safety-sensitive claims, electrical guidance, food/water guidance and jurisdiction-specific recommendations are not publication-ready until their sources and review dates are recorded.
