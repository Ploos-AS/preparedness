# Ploos Preparedness household baseline

All relevant Ploos Preparedness / Ploos Beredskap books share a common household-baseline model.

The baseline is not a universal shopping list. It is a set of domains that each jurisdiction and book can specialise without creating contradictory advice.

## Domains

Water; food; heat/clothing; light/energy; information/communications; payments; hygiene; medicines/first aid; and household-specific needs such as pets.

## Norway

Current Norwegian official guidance asks households to be prepared to manage for one week. DSB recommends storing about 20 litres of drinking water per person as the minimum weekly water need.

Payment resilience is broader than cash alone. Norges Bank recommends multiple payment cards, including a physical BankAxept card, some cash preferably in smaller denominations, and accounts in more than one bank.

There is deliberately no Ploos-defined universal cash amount. The household estimates likely necessary purchases after considering what it already stores.

## Book rule

A book may focus on a subset of the baseline, but it should not contradict it silently. If a title uses another duration or assumption for an exercise, that assumption must be explicit.

## Toolkit direction

`ploos-prep household` will combine the baseline with household inputs. `food` and `cash` remain planning tools rather than authoritative financial or nutritional advice.
