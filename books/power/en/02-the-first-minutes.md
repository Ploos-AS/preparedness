# The First Minutes

First determine whether the problem is limited to your home, affects the neighbourhood, or is wider. Do not turn a power cut into a more dangerous situation through improvised heating, open flames or unsafe electrical connections.

## First check

1. Use lighting intended for the purpose.
2. Check whether nearby buildings or street lighting have power.
3. Check breakers or protective devices only when it is safe to do so.
4. Preserve battery power on phones and communications equipment.
5. Avoid opening refrigerators and freezers unnecessarily.
6. Consider early how the home will retain heat.

Country-specific electrical-safety and official guidance will be explicitly sourced and dated before publication.
