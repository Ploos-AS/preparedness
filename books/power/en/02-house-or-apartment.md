# House or Apartment?

A useful power-cut plan depends on the building. A detached house with a chimney, stove, garage and outdoor space offers different options from an apartment without a fireplace or private outdoor area.

## House

A house may allow a certified wood-burning stove, more storage, a generator operated safely outdoors, or a fixed solar/battery installation. A larger building can also require considerably more energy to keep warm.

Identify a small part of the home that can become the warm zone. Keeping people comfortable in a limited area is often more realistic than maintaining normal temperatures throughout the building.

## Apartment

Storage, building rules, fire safety and access to alternative energy sources can be more restrictive. A combustion generator is generally not an apartment solution: exhaust and carbon monoxide require generators to remain outdoors and away from openings through which fumes could enter.

Apartment planning therefore puts greater emphasis on clothing, bedding and sleeping bags, battery power, lighting, low-cook food and an agreed alternative place to stay if the home becomes too cold.

National building, fire and fuel-storage rules vary across Europe and must be checked for the reader's jurisdiction.
