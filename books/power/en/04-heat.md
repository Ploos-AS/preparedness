# Heat

In winter, heat can be the difference between an inconvenient outage and a home that eventually becomes unsuitable to occupy.

Start by reducing demand: add clothing layers early, use blankets and sleeping bags, concentrate activity in a smaller warm zone, and close unused rooms. The first objective is to keep people warm rather than recreate normal temperatures throughout the building.

A certified wood-burning appliance and sound chimney can provide robust independent heat where available.

A portable gas heater may be an option only when the appliance is explicitly designed for indoor use. Ventilation, room size, clearances, supervision and the manufacturer's instructions matter. Appliances designed for outdoor cooking or heating must not be repurposed as indoor heaters.

If the home cannot be kept safely warm, moving to a pre-arranged warm location is part of the plan.
